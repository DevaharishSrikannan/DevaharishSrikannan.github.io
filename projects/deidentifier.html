All notes are fictional. A rule-based pipeline is transparent but brittle: the "Tricky cases" note shows names and dates it misses, which is why real de-identification adds trained models and human review.

Question

Can you share clinical notes safely?

Research and analytics teams need clinical text, but notes are full of names, dates, IDs and addresses. Under GDPR they must be removed or replaced before sharing.

  • Regular expressions for structured identifiers: Irish PPSN numbers, Eircodes, MRNs, phone numbers, emails and dates.
  • Context cues (titles like Dr or Mr) and a first-name dictionary for people.
  • Surrogate mode replaces each identifier with a consistent fake value, so the text stays readable.
Honest evaluation

Scored against labelled notes

Each sample note has hand-labelled identifiers. The page reports recall and precision live and lists exactly what was missed. The fourth note is built to break the rules, so the limits are visible rather than hidden.
