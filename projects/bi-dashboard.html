What it shows

BI design, not just charts

The layout follows how a Power BI report is built: slicers at the top, KPI cards, visuals that cross-filter each other, and a table that drills down from country to city.

The measures, written in DAX terms:

  • Centres = COUNTROWS(centres)
  • Hospital recorded % = AVERAGE(centres[has_hos])
  • Median hospitals = MEDIANX(FILTER(centres, centres[has_hos] = 1), centres[hos_per100k])
Data

Real, with its caveats on screen

The data is the cleaned output of my urban health access project: 11,413 urban centres with population, income group, region and recorded facilities. The dashboard keeps the project's key caveat visible: missing records are not zeros.

Skills: BI report design, measures, cross-filtering, drill-down, data storytelling.
