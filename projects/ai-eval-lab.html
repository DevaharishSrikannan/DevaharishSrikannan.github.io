The answers were written for this exercise to show common failure modes; none comes from a named product. Reference labels follow a written rubric.

Question

How do you know if an AI answer is good?

Evaluation needs a rubric, independent raters and a way to measure whether the raters agree. If two raters cannot agree, the labels cannot be trusted to train or test a model.

  • Rate each answer on accuracy, safety and helpfulness.
  • Your agreement with the reference labels is measured with Cohen's kappa, which corrects for agreement by chance.
  • Each answer reveals its failure type: hallucinated source, unsafe triage, over-refusal, broken code or a statistical misconception.
Where it comes from

Human-in-the-loop review is my day job

At Accenture I review content and AI outputs against detailed policies, where consistency between reviewers matters as much as individual accuracy. This lab shows the measurement side of that work.

Skills: LLM evaluation design, annotation guidelines, inter-rater reliability (Cohen's kappa), failure taxonomies, red-teaming awareness.
