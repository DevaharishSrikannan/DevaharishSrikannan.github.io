Research demo trained on paediatric images from one centre. It is not a medical device and must not be used for diagnosis.

Question

Does the model learn lungs or shortcuts?

The widely used Kermany chest X-ray dataset has several images per child, and its official folders are not guaranteed to be patient-disjoint. So before training, the notebook counts patients and byte-identical images shared across splits, rebuilds the split by patient and asserts that no patient appears twice.

  • Two-stage fine-tuning with class weighting, mixed precision and early stopping on validation AUROC.
  • Threshold chosen on validation only, then test AUROC, sensitivity and specificity with patient-level bootstrap intervals.
  • Grad-CAM on correct and incorrect cases.
Status

Code complete, training pending

The data handling is unit-tested (12 checks on parsing, leakage detection, splits and metrics). Results will be added from the notebook's own output, whatever they turn out to be.
