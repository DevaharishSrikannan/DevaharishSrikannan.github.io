ERBB2 Breast Cancer Expression

Differential expression, pathways and survival in TCGA. Compares ERBB2-amplified with non-amplified breast tumours using RNA-seq, copy-number and clinical data.

Question

How do ERBB2-amplified tumours differ?

RNA-seq, copy-number and clinical data from cBioPortal are matched by patient, and tumours are grouped by ERBB2 amplification status from the copy-number data.

  • DESeq2 normalisation and differential expression with multiple-testing correction.
  • Pathway enrichment with GO, KEGG and Reactome through clusterProfiler.
  • Variance-stabilised expression for PCA and heatmaps.
  • A regularised Cox model with glmnet linking expression to survival.
Tools

Written in R

DESeq2, clusterProfiler, glmnet, survival and survminer, with preprocessing and modelling kept in separate scripts.
