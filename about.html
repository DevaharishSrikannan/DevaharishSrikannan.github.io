About

From medical engineering to trustworthy health AI

I trained as a computer science and medical engineer in Chennai, then moved to Dublin for an MSc in AI for Medicine at UCD. Along the way I worked in research labs in India and Ireland, published four papers on AI in healthcare, and now work in Trust and Safety operations at Accenture, where judging quality and risk at scale is the daily job.

What ties it together: I like finding out whether a result is real. That means checking the data before the model, writing the statistics myself when it matters and saying plainly what a result does not show.
