About

I hold an MSc in Artificial Intelligence for Medicine and Medical Research from University College Dublin (2:1 equivalent) and a BTech in Computer Science and Medical Engineering. I like solving problems with data: Python, machine learning, deep learning, computer vision and generative AI, applied through research and technical projects.

At UCD's SPHERE Research Group I worked on data analysis, validation and technical reporting for healthcare research datasets. Today I work in Trust and Safety at Accenture, where I make evidence-based decisions on ambiguous cases and evaluate AI output quality, which has given me hands-on experience of human-in-the-loop AI.
