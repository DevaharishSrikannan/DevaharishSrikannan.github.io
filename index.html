How I work

Validated data, honest evaluation, results with their limits

Public health datasets hide traps. In my brain MRI work, two thirds of the official test images had a near-copy in training, which is why reported accuracy looked so good. In the urban health data, a blank count meant "not recorded", not "no hospital". I look for these problems first, then model.

I write the core statistics myself when it matters, test them on simulated data, and report negative results as clearly as positive ones.
