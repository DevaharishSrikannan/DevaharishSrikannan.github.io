Devaharish Srikannan

Data and AI researcher working at the intersection of health data science, machine learning and medical imaging. My experience spans data science research with UCD's SPHERE Research Group, research placements in Ireland and India, and high-accuracy Trust and Safety operations at Accenture UK & Ireland.

I hold an MSc in Artificial Intelligence for Medicine and Medical Research from University College Dublin (2H1) and have four publications on AI in healthcare. I focus on methods that hold up: validated data, honest evaluation and results reported with their limitations.

Open to data and AI roles, research positions and PhD opportunities.
